## Full description
**Never miss an expiry.**

Owly watches your passports, driver's licenses, visas, ID cards and insurance —
and nudges you *before* they expire. No spreadsheets, no last-minute panic at the
airport.

**How it works**
- Add any document in seconds — or snap a photo and let Owly read the expiry date
- Smart nudges: the right reminders at the right time, per document type
- Renewal checklists walk you through every renewal, step by step
- Renewal history keeps track of what you paid and when
- Track documents for the whole family — everyone gets their own coverage

**Private by design**
Your documents never leave your phone. No account, no cloud, no ads. Optional
app PIN protected by your phone's keychain.

**Made for humans**
Playful, warm, and simple enough for a kid to use — with all the power-ups tucked
one tap away for the rest of us.
